Does Varv keep going when my phone is locked?

Yes. Start a workout, lock your phone, switch to your music or put it in your pocket: every interval still starts on time, with its sound and haptic. Your whole workout shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Why does the Lock Screen count down the whole workout, not each interval?

iOS only lets an app change its Lock Screen card every interval if it runs a server. Varv works entirely on your phone and never goes online, so the card counts down the whole workout, with its rhythm of golden work and blue rest, and the sounds and haptics mark each interval.

Will the cues interrupt my music?

No. By default they play over your music without stopping it. To have the music dip briefly under each cue instead, choose Duck in Settings → Music.

Do cues play when my phone is on silent?

Yes, once you start a workout: you asked for them. Every cue also comes with a haptic, so you'll feel it with the sound down. (The quiet clicks of the timer builder's wheels follow the silent switch.)

Can Varv speak instead of playing tones?

Yes: Settings → Cues → Voice counts you in and names each interval. For the most natural voice, download the free "Enhanced" version: in the iPhone's Settings app, search for "Voices".

Can I start a workout with Siri?

Yes. Say "Start Tabata in Varv" (or any timer's name), or "Start my last workout in Varv". You can also start your last workout from a Home Screen widget, Control Center or the Action Button.

I swiped Varv away and my workout stopped.

That's on purpose: swiping an app away is how you tell iOS to stop it. To keep a workout going, just lock your phone or switch apps.
