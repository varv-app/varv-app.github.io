Privacy policy

Varv is an interval timer for iPhone. It collects no personal data.

  • No data collection. Varv has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It makes no network requests.
  • What stays on your phone. The timers you make and the workouts you finish are stored only on your iPhone (and in your device backups, if you use them). The developer never sees them.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and widgets. When you use Varv with Siri, Shortcuts, widgets or the Lock Screen, Apple's system features talk to the app on your phone. Varv doesn't send that information anywhere.
  • Sharing. If you share a workout image, you choose where it goes, using Apple's share sheet. Varv doesn't keep a copy elsewhere.
  • Children. Varv collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.
